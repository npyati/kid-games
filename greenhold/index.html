Greenhold

You are a knight of Greenhold castle. Talk to everyone, pick apples, and guard the castle from monsters!

All games
Xbox controllerLeft stick: walk · Right stick: look · A: talk, pick apples, or jump · RT: swing your sword · RB: shoot your bow · LT: run · X: eat an apple · Y or View: map · Start: menu
Keyboard and mouseWASD: walk · Mouse: look · Click: swing your sword · Right-click or R: shoot your bow · E: talk or pick apples · Space: jump · Shift: run · Q: eat an apple · M: map · Esc: menu
Your jobGo see the King in the throne room (first floor of the big keep). When the horn blows, monsters come out of the woods and march to the keep door. Stop them before they break it! The red arrow and the map show where they are. Guards on the walls shoot arrows, and knights fight too. Every 5th attack, a giant comes.
The castleThe keep has three floors and a roof. Floor 1: throne room and kitchen. Floor 2: banquet hall and library. Floor 3: the royal bedroom, the knights' beds and the armory. Take the stairs at the sides, or climb up onto the castle walls from the courtyard.
Gold and gearBeat monsters for gold. After each attack, the King gives you a reward. Spend your gold at Bram the blacksmith's forge in the courtyard for better swords, armor and a longbow. Ask a knight to come with you and they'll follow you and help.
FoodPick red apples from apple trees and berries from berry bushes (walk up and press A or E). Eat to heal. The butlers bring tea, the Queen has apple pie, and Cook Martha in the kitchen will buy your apples.
Getting knocked outIf you run out of health, you wake up in the knights' bunk room on floor 3 with full health. You keep all your stuff. Want a calm castle? Set Monsters to off.

Got an Xbox or PlayStation controller? Connect it to the computer (cable or Bluetooth), then press a button on it. Works best in Chrome or Edge. Not showing up? Close Steam, which can hide controllers, and try unplugging it and plugging it back in.
