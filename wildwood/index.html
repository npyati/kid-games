Wildwood

A forest that goes on forever. Walk anywhere, and look for the rivers and lakes.

All games
Xbox controllerLeft stick: walk · Right stick: look · A: jump · RT: swing · LT or click left stick: run · Y: backpack · Start: menu
Keyboard and mouseWASD: walk · Mouse: look · Click or F: swing · Space: jump · Shift: run · I or Tab: backpack · Esc: menu
AnimalsFoxes, deer and rabbits are friendly. Wolves, and bears from caves, chase you once they spot you. Beat them for gems, iron and apples. They can't swim!
CraftingSwing at trees for wood and at big rocks for stone. Open your backpack to make axes, swords and chest plates.
BossesGiant snakes hide in marshes. Wyverns (a kind of dragon) sleep in rare lairs on mountaintops, ringed by tall black spikes.

Plug in an Xbox controller and press any button.
