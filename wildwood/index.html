Wildwood

A forest that goes on forever. Walk anywhere, and look for the rivers and lakes.

All games
Xbox controllerLeft stick: walk · Right stick: look · A: jump · RT: swing or shoot · LB: block with a shield (you can still swing) · D-pad right: sword ↔ bow · D-pad left: shield on/off · LT or click left stick: run · Y: backpack · Start: menu
Keyboard and mouseWASD: walk · Mouse: look · Click or F: swing or shoot · Right-click or Q: block (you can still swing) · 1: sword · 2: bow · 3: shield on/off · Space: jump · Shift: run · I or Tab: backpack · Esc: menu
AnimalsFoxes, deer and rabbits are friendly. Wolves, and bears from caves, chase you once they spot you. Beat them for gems, iron and apples. They can't swim!
CraftingSwing at trees for wood and at big rocks for stone. Catch fish in rivers for their scales (golden fish are rare!). Open your backpack to make axes, swords, bows, shields and chest plates.
Saved gamesMake as many games as you like with 💾 Saved games. Each one has its own world and its own things. New game starts from scratch.
BossesDragonlings live in forest camps: leave them alone and they'll leave you alone. Crocodiles lurk in rivers. Sharks swim in the ocean, but only bother you in the water. Goats (leave them alone!) and ice ogres live in the icy mountains. Goblins camp in open fields. Ogres roam the forests. Giant snakes hide in marshes. The Leviathan lives in big lakes. Dragons sleep in volcanoes on mountaintops, ringed by tall black spikes. They walk on their folded wings, and they can fly!

Plug in an Xbox controller and press any button.
