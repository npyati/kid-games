How to play

1. Walk to the end of the long pier. 2. Cast your line and watch your hook sink through the zones of the ocean. 3. When it stops, a fish from that zone bites. Hold to reel it in, and let go when it thrashes so the line doesn't pull too hard.

Every fish goes into your Aquarium (the giant glass dome), and every fish there makes coins every second, even while you're fishing. Spend coins at the Tackle Shop on heavier sinkers to go deeper, faster reels, and luckier bait.

Every zone has eight creatures: four common ones, two rare ones and two mythical ones. The deepest zone hides the legendary King Leviathan. Deeper zones have fish worth much more. Press Stop here on the way down to fish a shallower zone.

Big creatures are life size! They're too heavy to hold up, so you get a picture of them lying on the beach next to you.

Controls

Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to walk, Q and E (or drag with the mouse) to turn the camera, Space to cast, stop and reel (hold), Esc for the menu.

Mouse or touch: tap the ground to walk there, drag to turn the camera, tap the yellow button to cast, hold anywhere to reel.

Xbox controller: left stick to walk, right stick to turn the camera, A to cast, stop and reel (hold A or RT), B to go back, Y for the Fishdex, Start for the menu.
