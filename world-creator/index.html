How to play

Fly the cameraController: left stick moves, right stick turns and tilts, LT zooms out, RT zooms in. Keyboard and mouse: WASD or the arrow keys move, hold the right mouse button and drag to turn, mouse wheel zooms, Q / E turn.
BuildPick a group and a thing at the bottom, then press A or click to put it down where the circle is. Hold the button and move to keep going. Controller: LB / RB change group, d-pad left / right pick a thing, click the right stick to change its size, Y turns it. Keyboard: number keys or Tab change group, [ and ] pick a thing, R turns it, - and + change its size.
BrushThe brush on the left side puts down 1, 2, 3, 6 or 10 things at a time, and makes the circle bigger for painting land. Controller: d-pad up / down. Keyboard: C (Shift+C for smaller).
NaturePaint land types: forest, taiga, jungle, savanna, desert, swamp, mountain, path, cobblestone path and corrupted land. Each one colours the ground and grows its own plants. Hold the button to keep painting. The land tools raise and lower the land, flatten it, or dig shallow or deep water. Single trees, rocks and flowers are in Plants.
Oops!The eraser (X on the controller or keyboard) takes things away. Undo (B, or Z on the keyboard) takes back the last thing you did.
It's alivePeople and creatures walk around the island by themselves. Birds and dragons fly, and fish swim in the sea.
SavingEvery island saves by itself. Find it again under My islands.
