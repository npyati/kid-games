How to play

ControllersXbox and PlayStation controllers both work. On PlayStation, ✕ is A, ◯ is B, ▢ is X, △ is Y, L1 / R1 are LB / RB, L2 / R2 are LT / RT, Create is View and Options is Start. The hints at the bottom of the screen use your controller's names.
Fly the cameraController: left stick moves, right stick turns and tilts, LT zooms out, RT zooms in. Keyboard and mouse: WASD or the arrow keys move, hold the right mouse button and drag to turn, mouse wheel zooms, Q / E turn.
BuildPick a group and a thing at the bottom, then press A or click to put it down where the circle is. Hold the button and move to keep going. Controller: LB / RB change group, d-pad left / right pick a thing, click the right stick to change its size, Y turns it. Keyboard: number keys or Tab change group, [ and ] pick a thing, R turns it, - and + change its size.
BrushThe brush on the left side puts down 1, 2, 3, 6 or 10 things at a time, and makes the circle bigger for painting land. Controller: d-pad up / down. Keyboard: C (Shift+C for smaller).
NaturePaint land types: forest, taiga, jungle, savanna, desert, swamp, mountain, path, cobblestone path, corrupted land and glowing lava (it burns what's on it, and nobody walks onto it). Each one colours the ground and grows its own plants. Hold the button to keep painting. The land tools raise and lower the land, flatten it, or dig shallow or deep water. Trees and plants grow by themselves on the land types you paint.
Oops!The eraser (X on the controller or keyboard) takes things away. Undo (B, or Z on the keyboard) takes back the last thing you did.
It's alivePeople and creatures walk around by themselves. Birds and dragons fly, and dolphins swim in the sea. Monsters, zombies and the dragon attack; zombies turn what they bite into zombies; anything near a ghost runs away screaming. Turn Fighting off in the menu for calm building.
TownsPut down humans, elves, dwarves or orcs (in People). With Towns: People build (in the menu), once 10 of a kind are together they start building a town: the town hall takes about 2 minutes, then they build their own houses one at a time. With Towns: Quick, one person founds a whole town at once. Orcs fight everyone else; humans sometimes start wars; elves are archers. Open 🏰 Towns (T, or View on the controller) to make a town keep the peace or go to war with everyone.
MapsTen islands, plus four big maps: Island Set (seven islands, each a different land), Pangea (one giant supercontinent), Sky Islands (floating in the sky) and Endless Ocean. Or pick 🎲 Random. New islands are much bigger than the first ones, and people and animals are smaller, so everything is more to scale.
LegendaryThe ⭐ Legendary group has Cerberus (a three-headed dog that bites with one head while the others snarl), the ice dragon (its frost freezes things and puts out fires), the lightning dragon (lightning bolts that jump from one thing to the next), the water dragon (a water jet that pushes things back and puts out fires), the golden dragon (golden fire, and the toughest of all) and the Roc, a giant eagle.
Conspiracy TheoriesThe 🛸 Conspiracy Theories group has the Loch Ness Monster (in deep water, with a very long neck and a jaw full of teeth), the Kraken (in deep water: its tentacles wrap around whatever it catches and lift it out of the water), Bigfoot, the Alien (with a blaster that shoots glowing bolts) and the Chupacabra.
Shop and achievementsThe 🛒 Shop (top right, or in the Menu) sells the Conspiracy Theories and Legendary packs, the Cosmic Dragon, the Killer robot, the Alien robot, the Owlbear, special effects and skies. Earn 🪙 15 coins every time you complete an achievement: there are 150 of them (50 things to do, each at three levels), and you can complete each one again and again. See them under 🏆 Achievements in the Shop.
Fire and rainIn a fight, a dragon sometimes breathes fire instead of biting. Its fire burns what it hits and sets fires where it lands: fires burn trees, plants and buildings down slowly, hurt anyone standing in them, can spread to plants nearby, and burn out after about a minute. With Fighting off, a dragon just breathes fire at the sky now and then. The ✨ Special effects group has 🌧️ Rain: a dark cloud rains for 20 seconds (bigger with size) and puts out every fire under it.
DisastersThe Disasters group has a tornado (picks things up and throws them), a tsunami (a wave rolls in from the nearest sea), a bomb, a sprinkle bomb (lots of little bombs) and an earthquake (cracks the ground and knocks buildings down). The size buttons make them bigger. Undo puts things back.
Kings, queens and battlesOnce a town starts to grow it crowns a king, a queen or both. Their town hall becomes a castle, and they live inside it in fancy robes, coming out for a walk now and then and whenever their town is at war. A golden crown treasure near the town makes their crowns grander. They train soldiers in armour who stand guard around the town and go to battle. Ordinary townsfolk never fight: they run from danger. Tap the 🪄 Battle wand (in People) on one town, then on another, and they go to war. The 🕊️ Peace wand does the opposite: the two towns become friends and never fight (even orcs). Bears, polar bears, foxes, crocodiles, sharks and dinosaurs are hungry: they hunt people and other animals. When a town has nobody left, its town hall explodes and it has fallen.
Farms and cavesTowns plant crop fields: farmers walk out, hoe the ground and bring in the harvest (40 gold each time), and barns come with cows, sheep, pigs or chickens. New islands start with trees and plants that fit them, and some have caves that animals (or goblins) come out of.
TreasureThe 💰 Treasure group has gold bags (small, medium, big and huge), treasure chests, a pile of gold, a golden crown, giant gems and gold dragon and hydra statues. Put treasure near a town and it goes into the town's gold: gold bags get spent right away, and the rest stays in the town. Bigger treasure is worth more.
InspectPress 🔍 Inspect (I on the keyboard, or press X twice on the controller): nothing gets built while you're inspecting. Click a person, an animal, a monster or a town. People and animals have a name, an age, a personality, a job and a story of what's happened to them. Towns show how much money they have, their rulers, whether they're at war, at peace or neutral, and their history. One year goes by every 2 minutes. Making water washes away the trees, plants and buildings it covers (treasure stays, sunken!), and land creatures that end up in the water swim slowly back to shore. Kids are born in the towns and grow up; only people 18 or older can have a job (farmer, builder, soldier, king or queen) or go to war.
Big monstersDragons (of every kind), hydras, sea serpents, Loch Ness Monsters and Krakens are huge, and each island can have up to 3 of each.
SavingEvery island saves by itself. Find it again under My islands.
