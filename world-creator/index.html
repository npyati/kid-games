How to play

ControllersXbox and PlayStation controllers both work. On PlayStation, ✕ is A, ◯ is B, ▢ is X, △ is Y, L1 / R1 are LB / RB, L2 / R2 are LT / RT, Create is View and Options is Start. The hints at the bottom of the screen use your controller's names.
Fly the cameraController: left stick moves, right stick turns and tilts, LT zooms out, RT zooms in. Keyboard and mouse: WASD or the arrow keys move, hold the right mouse button and drag to turn, mouse wheel zooms, Q / E turn.
BuildPick a group and a thing at the bottom, then press A or click to put it down where the circle is. Hold the button and move to keep going. Controller: LB / RB change group, d-pad left / right pick a thing, click the right stick to change its size, Y turns it. Keyboard: number keys or Tab change group, [ and ] pick a thing, R turns it, - and + change its size.
BrushThe brush on the left side puts down 1, 2, 3, 6 or 10 things at a time, and makes the circle bigger for painting land. Controller: d-pad up / down. Keyboard: C (Shift+C for smaller).
NaturePaint land types: forest, taiga, jungle, savanna, desert, swamp, mountain, path, cobblestone path, corrupted land and glowing lava (it burns what's on it, and nobody walks onto it). Each one colours the ground and grows its own plants. Hold the button to keep painting. The land tools raise and lower the land, flatten it, or dig shallow or deep water. Single trees, rocks and flowers are in Plants.
Oops!The eraser (X on the controller or keyboard) takes things away. Undo (B, or Z on the keyboard) takes back the last thing you did.
It's alivePeople and creatures walk around by themselves. Birds and dragons fly, and dolphins swim in the sea. Monsters, zombies and the dragon attack; zombies turn what they bite into zombies; anything near a ghost runs away screaming. Turn Fighting off in the menu for calm building.
TownsPut down humans, elves, dwarves or orcs (in People). With Towns: People build (in the menu), once 10 of a kind are together they start building a town: the town hall takes about 2 minutes, then they build their own houses one at a time. With Towns: Quick, one person founds a whole town at once. Orcs fight everyone else; humans sometimes start wars; elves are archers. Open 🏰 Towns (T, or View on the controller) to make a town keep the peace or go to war with everyone.
MapsTen islands, plus four big maps: Island Set (seven islands, each a different land), Pangea (one giant supercontinent), Sky Islands (floating in the sky) and Endless Ocean. Or pick 🎲 Random. New islands are much bigger than the first ones, and people and animals are smaller, so everything is more to scale.
DisastersThe Disasters group has a tornado (picks things up and throws them), a tsunami (a wave rolls in from the nearest sea), a bomb, a sprinkle bomb (lots of little bombs) and an earthquake (cracks the ground and knocks buildings down). The size buttons make them bigger. Undo puts things back.
Kings, queens and battlesOnce a town starts to grow it crowns a king, a queen or both. They train soldiers who guard the town and send them to battle. Tap the 🪄 Battle wand (in People) on one town, then on another, and they go to war. When a town has nobody left, its town hall explodes and it has fallen.
Big monstersDragons, hydras and sea serpents are huge, and each island can have up to 3 of each.
SavingEvery island saves by itself. Find it again under My islands.
