How to play

RaceYour chicken runs by itself. Race three rival chickens to the finish flag and try to come 1st! Each world has its own rivals.
Jump and flapPress Space, the Up arrow, or tap the screen to jump. Press again in the air to flap higher.
Xbox controllerPress A (or any face button) to jump and flap. Start pauses. In menus, use the stick or d-pad to move, A to choose and B to go back. LB and RB flip pages of worlds and skins.
BumpsBumping into something makes you stumble, which costs time. Finish fast to earn more stars, and finish 1st, 2nd or 3rd for extra bonus coins.
Good stuff (gold glow)
Yucky stuff (purple glow): jump over it or stay low
Coins and skinsCoins you grab are yours to keep. Spend them on over 2,000 skins in the Skin Shop. Finish a world to unlock the next one: after the first six come 500 bonus worlds!
